# Crawling policy.
#
# Search crawlers and the bots that fetch a page to answer a live question are welcome:
# being quotable is the point of publishing this. Crawlers that collect a corpus for
# model training are not — the site documents a way of working, it does not donate it.
#
# The split is by purpose, not by company: OpenAI, Anthropic and Perplexity each run one
# crawler for their index and another for training, and the two are treated differently.
# All of this is honoured voluntarily; robots.txt asks, it does not enforce.
