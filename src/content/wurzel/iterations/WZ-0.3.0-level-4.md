summary:
  en: "A level that exists because of a test session, not because a feature was missing. Three manual walks of the Level-3 checklist produced a body of defects with a delivery order of its own — too large to absorb into the level that produced them without breaking the 25-story cap, and too structural to leave in a backlog. Block 1 fixes the first walk's findings, severest first: every invoice exported so far carried a permanently dateless PDF, because the document model was built before the date was stamped. Block 2 fixes the second walk's, and in doing so turns a repeated one-off fix into a written rule — preview and export save first, on every surface, and run only if that save succeeded. Block 3 fixes the third walk's, including the one finding that reversed a decision rather than repairing a defect: billing above a position's remaining quantity is no longer refused, it raises the position. All 16 stories in this iteration are Done, and each of the three blocks has been walked; the level was signed off on 2026-09-16."

processChanges:
  en:
    - "The human walk moved from the end of the pipeline to the front of every block. `/deliver-level` was replaced by `/deliver-block`, which stops after each block for me to open the application, and a finding against a criterion that block delivered is fixed inside the level rather than groomed into a later one. Level 3 named the gap this closes: its first walk came four days after the last story had landed, and by then eighteen findings — several of them legally defective, byte-frozen documents — had already shipped past a green suite and a clean dual agent review."
    - "Two rules were written that agents cannot talk their way around. A criterion counts as covered only when a test enters where the user enters — the lost settings autosave had a fully green suite over it because the tests called the save method directly and none simulated navigating away. And any story that freezes, locks, exports, cancels, supersedes or autosaves answers four state-transition questions in writing, because Level 3's severe findings all lived between stories that each passed their own review."
    - "The seam check is deliberately structural rather than advisory. The first draft of the fix wrote the questions down as a rule for the product-owner agent to apply, and that was rejected: Level 3's Block 1 had been grilled, and the question of what cancelling an advance does to a frozen final invoice still never reached me. So the check became a sixth axis of the consistency pass whose blocking findings are phrased as questions and carried to me at the readiness gate — and the product-owner agent is barred from answering one itself."

lessons:
  en:
    - "Agent review is not a walk. Agents verified the acceptance criteria against the code; nobody verified the code against the running application. A dateless invoice PDF, a state badge rendered twice per row and a fully-billed position hidden where the story said greyed out all passed dual adversarial review, and every one of them is visible within seconds of clicking. The review was not sloppy — it was answering a different question than the one that mattered."
    - "A test can cover a criterion completely and prove nothing. The suite was green over the lost settings autosave because the tests called the save method directly, and none of them navigated away from the page. Coverage on paper and coverage in fact are different measurements, and only the second one is about the user. That is now a rule: enter where the user enters."
    - "A check that depends on being remembered is not a check. Writing the seam questions down as guidance for the reviewing agent would have failed the same way it already had — the block that missed the question had been grilled thoroughly. Making the questions a blocking axis that must be carried to a human, with the agent forbidden from answering them, is what turns the intention into a mechanism."
  de:
    - "Eine Agenten-Review ist kein Durchgang. Agenten prüften die Akzeptanzkriterien gegen den Code; niemand prüfte den Code gegen die laufende Anwendung. Ein Rechnungs-PDF ohne Datum, ein zweimal je Zeile gerendertes Status-Kennzeichen und eine vollständig abgerechnete Position, die ausgeblendet wurde, wo die Story ausgegraut verlangt — alles bestand die doppelte adversariale Review, und jedes davon ist in Sekunden Klickens sichtbar. Die Review war nicht nachlässig: Sie beantwortete eine andere Frage als die, auf die es ankam."
    - "Ein Test kann ein Kriterium vollständig abdecken und nichts beweisen. Über dem verlorenen Autosave in den Einstellungen war die Suite grün, weil die Tests die Speichermethode direkt aufriefen und keiner die Seite verließ. Abdeckung auf dem Papier und Abdeckung in der Sache sind zwei verschiedene Messungen, und nur die zweite handelt von der Nutzerin. Daraus ist eine Regel geworden: dort einsteigen, wo die Nutzerin einsteigt."
    - "Eine Prüfung, die daran hängt, dass jemand an sie denkt, ist keine Prüfung. Die Nahtstellen-Fragen als Hinweis für den prüfenden Agenten aufzuschreiben wäre genauso gescheitert wie zuvor — der Block, der die Frage verpasst hat, war gründlich gegrillt worden. Erst dass die Fragen eine blockierende Achse sind, die zwingend zu einem Menschen getragen wird, und dass der Agent sie nicht selbst beantworten darf, macht aus der Absicht einen Mechanismus."
aiContribution:
  en: "This is the iteration where the AI was wrong in public and where it was useful in a way no walk could be — both worth recording. It was wrong about the cap on billing above a position's remaining quantity: the arithmetic was right and the rule was unusable at the kerb, and the tester, not the machine, reported the guard itself as the defect. It was wrong, more quietly, about its own verification: the same review that proposed the invoice chain's mechanisms also certified them, and it certified a legally dateless PDF. Where it was genuinely useful was after each walk, reading the codebase rather than waiting for the next session to trip over the same seam one surface at a time — it found every remaining place where an action read the stored record while the screen held something newer, and turned five would-be stories into one written rule. It also found a failure no walk could report: an export refused on the latest-offer card showed its error to nobody, because the card never bound the event, and a silent failure produces nothing to report. And in the specifications it caught what a person reading one story at a time cannot — that suppressing the table reconcile during a save would also have stopped the client learning what the server changed, and that a blank description on a deactivated row is legal on screen and fatal on the wire."
  de: "Das ist die Iteration, in der die KI öffentlich falsch lag und in der sie auf eine Weise nützlich war, wie es kein Durchgang sein kann — beides gehört festgehalten. Falsch lag sie bei der Begrenzung, die mehr abzurechnen verweigerte, als auf einer Position übrig war: Die Rechnung stimmte, und die Regel war am Bordstein unbrauchbar; als Fehler gemeldet hat die Absicherung selbst der Tester, nicht die Maschine. Stiller falsch lag sie bei ihrer eigenen Prüfung: Dieselbe Review, die die Mechanismen der Rechnungsstrecke vorschlug, hat sie auch bescheinigt — und sie bescheinigte ein rechtlich datumsloses PDF. Wirklich nützlich war sie nach jedem Durchgang, indem sie den Code las, statt zu warten, bis die nächste Sitzung über dieselbe Naht Oberfläche für Oberfläche stolpert: Sie fand jede verbliebene Stelle, an der eine Aktion auf den gespeicherten Datensatz wirkte, während der Bildschirm etwas Neueres hielt, und machte aus fünf möglichen Stories eine geschriebene Regel. Sie fand außerdem einen Fehlschlag, den kein Durchgang melden konnte: Ein auf der Karte des neuesten Angebots abgelehnter Export zeigte seinen Fehler niemandem, weil die Karte das Ereignis nie gebunden hatte — und ein stiller Fehlschlag erzeugt nichts, was zu melden wäre.
